# Research agenda

## Central question
**How do distance, turbidity, transmit power, and noise floor change a simplified underwater optical link budget and OOK BER?**

## Hypotheses
### H1
Turbidity shortens usable range exponentially under Beer-Lambert attenuation.

### H2
BER changes sharply near the low-SNR operating boundary.

### H3
Parameter sensitivity is large enough that field calibration will be essential before deployment claims.

## Experiments
1. Generate distance-vs-received-power and distance-vs-BER curves for all water presets.
2. Sweep noise floor and transmit power to map feasible regions.
3. Later compare model predictions with any legitimate laboratory measurements and report residual error.

## Metrics
- received power
- SNR
- BER
- distance to chosen BER threshold
- parameter sensitivity
