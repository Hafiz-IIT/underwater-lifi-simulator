# Evaluation

## Question
How do distance, turbidity and noise change the feasible operating region of a simplified underwater optical link?

## Metrics
- Received power
- SNR
- BER
- Estimated usable range
- Sensitivity to attenuation/noise

## Falsification
- Power increases with distance.
- Turbid water outperforms clear water under identical parameters without explanation.
- Lower SNR produces lower BER.
